\begin{abstract}
The Carry Independence Conjecture (CIC) of arXiv:2603.11066 --- every
odd $n_0>1$ eventually takes a Syracuse letter $b\ge3$ --- is the
weakest nontrivial case of our two-clock calibration conjecture (it
asserts the handoff parity cannot equal $1$ forever). We attack it
with the lifting-the-exponent machinery. Results: (1) convergent
orbits satisfy the CIC automatically (every entry into $1$ uses a
letter $b\ge4$), so the CIC concerns only divergent $\{1,2\}$-orbits;
(2) a $\{1,2\}$-orbit is exactly an iterated chain of odd integers in
which each survival event is the LTE reading
$\vt(3^\ell m-1)=\vt(m-3^{-\ell})$ --- alignment of the orbit's fresh
high bits to a \emph{rotating $2$-adic target} --- with death iff the
reading is odd; (3) under Haar measure the round process is
\emph{exactly} i.i.d.: survival probability $\tfrac13$ per round,
geometric laws on every fiber (proved via the census lemma; the
freshness slope is $2\cdot3^{\#\text{letters}}$), and surviving rounds
drift \emph{upward} at $+0.6166$ bits per round in the mean --- the
apparent anti-correlations in finite enumerations are truncation
artifacts; (4) Mersenne points $2^g-1$ survive their first round iff
$g$ is odd and $\vt(g-1)$ is even (pure LTE, deterministic); (5) the
$\{1,2\}$ alphabet has an \emph{entropy deficit}: confined-word growth
is bounded by $2^{H(2-\log_23)}=1.9712<2$ at \emph{every} strip width,
so the narrow-band dwell machinery applies to CIC-violators with no
width cutoff --- the allowed region of the $(D,\varepsilon)$ phase
diagram is empty for them; (6) empirically, the least integer
surviving $D$ letters grows like $2^{D/2.2}$. The remaining wall is a
single scalar parity sequence read from provably fresh bits.
\end{abstract}

\section{Reduction to divergent $\{1,2\}$-orbits}

\begin{lemma}[Convergent orbits satisfy the CIC]\label{lem:conv}
If the Syracuse orbit of $n_0>1$ reaches $1$, it takes a letter
$b\ge4$ at its last step before $1$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$S(y)=1$ means $3y+1=2^b$, i.e.\ $y=(4^k-1)/3$ with $b=2k$ (only even
$b$ give integers). $k=1$ is $y=1$; every entry from $y\ne1$ has
$b=2k\ge4$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:red}
A CIC-violator is either a nontrivial positive cycle with all letters
in $\{1,2\}$ --- excluded in all computationally verified ranges, and
period-pinned by the Baker mechanism of the narrow-band note --- or a
\emph{divergent} orbit with all letters in $\{1,2\}$. The CIC is
henceforth the statement that no divergent $\{1,2\}$-orbit exists.
\end{corollary}

\section{The round chain and its LTE normal form}

\begin{theorem}[Round chain]\label{thm:chain}
Let $x\equiv3\pmod4$ be a $1$-run start, $g=\vt(x+1)$, $\ell=g-1$,
$m=(x+1)/2^g$ odd. Set $v=\vt(3^\ell m-1)$. Then the next letters of
$x$ are exactly $1^\ell\,2^{\lfloor v/2\rfloor}$ followed by: a letter
$b\ge3$ if $v$ is odd (\emph{death}), or a new $1$-run if $v$ is even
(\emph{survival}), in which case, with $r=v/2$ and
$u=(3^\ell m-1)/2^v$,
\[
m'=\frac{3^ru+1}{2^{\,\vt(3^ru+1)}},\qquad
\ell'=\vt(3^ru+1),
\]
are the parameters of the next round. Moreover the reading has the
LTE normal form
\[
v=\vt(3^\ell m-1)=\vt\bigl(m-3^{-\ell}\bigr):
\]
each survival event is an alignment reading of the fresh multiplier
$m$ against the rotating $2$-adic target $3^{-\ell}\in\mathbb Z_2^\times$.
(Machine-verified: $5000$ rounds, letters, death criterion and
$(g',m')$ all exact; LTE form on $2000$ random $(\ell,m)$.)
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The $-1$ clock gives the run $1^\ell$ ending at $y$ with
$y-1=2(3^\ell m-1)$, so $h=\vt(y-1)=1+v$; the $+1$ clock gives the
$2$-run of length $\lfloor(h-1)/2\rfloor=\lfloor v/2\rfloor$ with
parity handoff: $h$ odd ($v$ even) hands to a $1$-run, $h$ even ($v$
odd) forces $b\ge3$. The terminal point is $z$ with
$z+1=2(3^ru+1)$, giving $(g',m')$. The normal form is
$\vt(3^\ell)\,{=}\,0$ plus invertibility of $3$ in $\mathbb Z_2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The CIC is thus: \emph{no odd integer can feed the chain even
readings forever}. The targets $3^{-\ell}$ rotate through the index-$4$
subgroup $\langle3\rangle\subset(\mathbb Z/2^j)^\times$, and at scale
$j$ the target depends only on $\ell\bmod2^{j-2}$ --- the
lifting-the-exponent structure.
\end{remark}

\section{Exact Haar laws: the survival coin is i.i.d.}

\begin{theorem}[Freshness; exact i.i.d.\ laws]\label{thm:iid}
Condition on the full data of rounds $1,\dots,i$ (all letters and all
read bits). Then the round-$(i{+}1)$ reading is exactly geometric:
$\mathbb P[v=k]=2^{-k}$ $(k\ge1)$, hence
\[
\mathbb P[\text{survive round}]=\sum_{k\ge2\ \mathrm{even}}2^{-k}
=\tfrac13
\]
independently across rounds; given survival,
$\mathbb P[r=t]=3\cdot4^{-t}$ ($\mathbb E[r]=\tfrac43$) and the next
depth is $\mathbb P[g'=j]=2^{-(j-1)}$ ($\mathbb E[\ell']=2$).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Write the unread part of $m$ as $m=a+2^{w}s$ with $s$ the free (Haar)
parameter. Through the round, every derived quantity is affine in $s$
with slope of the exact form $2\cdot3^{B}\cdot s$ ($B$ = letters
consumed): $u=u_0+2\cdot3^{\ell}s$, $3^ru+1=T_0+2\cdot3^{\ell+r}s$,
and after extracting $2^{g'}$ and conditioning, $m'=m_0'+2\cdot3^{B}s'$
--- the same form, renormalized. The census lemma of the ghost-clock
note (slope valuation $\gamma=1\le\beta$) then gives the exact
geometric law of each reading on each fiber, by induction over
rounds. (This is the orbit-parameterization slope $2\cdot3^{D}$ of
arXiv:2603.11066 composed with the census lemma; it generalizes that
manuscript's Carry Contamination Theorem from mod-$8$ equidistribution
of one round to exact i.i.d.\ laws of the whole process.)
\end{proof}

\noindent\emph{Verification.} Exact enumeration of all $1$-run-start
residue classes modulo $2^{24}$, counting only classes whose round
outcomes are class-determined: round-$1$ survival
$1398024/4194144=0.333328$, round-$2$ $0.332689$. Deeper rounds show
$0.322$, $0.272$, $0.125$ --- this is \emph{truncation bias}, not
anti-correlation: the determinedness filter caps the current reading
at the remaining bit budget, and a geometric law truncated at an odd
cutoff has even-mass below $\tfrac13$ (e.g.\ $2/7$ at cutoff $3$). Any
empirical ``two-round anti-correlation'' at finite modulus has this
origin; under Haar the coin is exactly fair-thirds and i.i.d.

\begin{corollary}[Survivors ascend]\label{cor:ascend}
Conditioned on survival, the mean drift per round is
\[
(\log_23-1)\,\mathbb E[\ell]-(2-\log_23)\,\mathbb E[r]
=2(0.58496)-\tfrac43(0.41504)=+0.6166\ \text{bits},
\]
i.e.\ $+0.185$ bits per letter (verified: $+0.6164$ over the
enumerated survivor classes). Haar-typical points die (density
$(1/3)^j$ after $j$ rounds, so $\mathcal C_\infty$ is a measure-zero
Cantor set), while the surviving fibers drift upward at a definite
rate: a positive CIC-violator, if one exists, lives in an exponentially
thin set \emph{and} is statistically forced toward divergence ---
consistent with \cref{cor:red}.
\end{corollary}

\section{Deterministic sub-families: Mersenne LTE}

\begin{theorem}[Mersenne first round]\label{thm:mersenne}
For $x=2^g-1$ ($m=1$, $\ell=g-1$) the reading is fully deterministic:
$v=\vt(3^{g-1}-1)$ equals $1$ if $g$ is even, and $2+\vt(g-1)$ if $g$
is odd (LTE). Hence $2^g-1$ survives its first round iff $g$ is odd
and $\vt(g-1)$ is even. (Verified $g\le60$: survivors
$g\in\{5,13,17,21,29,37,45,49,53\}$, i.e.\
$g-1\equiv2^{2t}\bmod2^{2t+1}$.)
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The truncated ghosts are precisely the points whose readings are
\emph{not} fresh --- and there LTE decides everything. Freshness
(\cref{thm:iid}) and LTE determinism (\cref{thm:mersenne}) are the two
poles of the problem: the wall is exactly the regime between them.
\end{remark}

\section{The entropy deficit of the $\{1,2\}$ alphabet}

\begin{theorem}[Entropy deficit]\label{thm:deficit}
Let $N^{12}_D(p)$ count $D$-confined valuation words of length $p$
over the alphabet $\{1,2\}$, and
$\lambda_{12}(D)=\lim N^{12}_D(p)^{1/p}$. Then for \emph{every} $D$,
\[
\theta_{12}(D)=\log_2\lambda_{12}(D)\ \le\ H(2-\log_23)=0.97907<1 ,
\]
since confinement pins the letter-$1$ frequency at $2-\log_23$ and
binary words at fixed frequency number at most
$2^{H(q)p+o(p)}$. Exact quantized counts (rotation-driven recursion,
$p=2000$): $\theta_{12}=0.521,\ 0.753,\ 0.889,\ 0.955,\ 0.973$ at
$D=0.8,\ 1.2,\ 2,\ 4,\ 8$, increasing toward the ceiling.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[All-width dwell for CIC-violators]\label{cor:dwell}
An acyclic $\{1,2\}$-orbit dwells at most
$C\,(WY)^{0.980}$ consecutive steps in \emph{any} band $[Y,WY]$ ---
including arbitrarily wide bands. (Proof: repetition rigidity plus the
pigeonhole of the narrow-band theorem, with $N^{12}$ in place of $N$;
no $D^*$ cutoff because $\theta_{12}<1$ uniformly.) In particular, a
single excursion of a divergent $\{1,2\}$-orbit below height $X$ lasts
at most $C\,X^{0.980}$ steps, and the allowed (red) region of the
$(D,\varepsilon)$ phase diagram is \emph{empty} for CIC-violators: the
full-alphabet escape hatch at $D>D^*=1.246$ is closed by the missing
letters.
\end{corollary}

\section{Survival champions}

The least $n_0\equiv3\pmod4$ surviving $D$ letters (seeds to
$2^{26}$):
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccc}
\toprule
D & 4\text{--}19 & 20 & 28 & 36 & 39\text{--}44\\
\midrule
\min n_0 & 27 & 4591 & 31911 & 517191 & 687871\\
D/\log_2 n_0 & \le4.0 & 1.64 & 1.87 & 1.90 & 2.0\text{--}2.3\\
\bottomrule
\end{array}
\]
The ratio $D/\log_2(\min n_0)$ stabilizes near $2$: empirically the
least survivor of depth $D$ has height about $2^{D/2.2}$. We record:

\begin{conjecture}[Champion growth]\label{conj:champ}
There is $c>0$ with $\min\{n_0:\text{$n_0$ survives $D$ letters}\}\ge
2^{cD}$. Granting this, every CIC-violating orbit satisfies
$x_n\ge2^{c'n}$: violators must flee to infinity at a definite
exponential rate, closing the CIC for all sub-exponential orbits.
\end{conjecture}

\section{The $3$-smooth cascade and the $2$-adic logarithm calculus}

The Mersenne theorem extends to the full $3$-smooth neighborhoods
$x=2^g3^a\mp1$ --- and the cascade goes \emph{deeper} than one round.
All statements below are machine-verified
(\texttt{search/smooth\_cascade.py}; grids $g<42$, $a<26$, and the
one-parameter family to $n\le3000$).

\begin{theorem}[Minus family]\label{thm:minus}
For $x=2^g3^a-1$ ($g\ge2$) the entire round chain depends on $(g,a)$
only through $n=g+a-1$ (the chain sees $m,\ell$ only through
$3^\ell m=3^n$). The first reading is $v=\vt(3^n-1)$, given by LTE:
$v=1$ for $n$ odd, $v=2+\vt(n)$ for $n$ even; survival iff $\vt(n)$
is even $\ge2$, of density $\tfrac16$ over $n$ --- the family is
ghost-aligned and dies at $2\times$ the Haar rate in round one. The
round-$2$ reading is the $4$-term $S$-unit valuation
\[
v'=\vt\bigl(3^{\,n+r+\ell'}-3^{\,r+\ell'}+2^{v}3^{\ell'}-2^{\,v+\ell'}\bigr)
-v-\ell' .
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Plus family]\label{thm:plus}
For $x=2^g3^a+1$ ($g\ge3$) the $+1$ clock rides
$\lfloor(g-1)/2\rfloor$ blocks ending $3$-smooth, with
$a'=a+\lfloor(g-1)/2\rfloor$:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] $g$ even: terminal $4\cdot3^{a'}+1$, death letter
$b=2+\vt(3^{a'+1}+1)\in\{3,4\}$ by LTE --- closed-form CIC death.
\item[(ii)] $g$ odd: lands at $2\cdot3^{a'}+1$, next depth $g'=2$
($a'$ even) or $3$ ($a'$ odd). If $a'$ is even the round-$2$ reading
is $v'=\vt(3^{a'+1}+1)-1=1$: closed-form death. If $a'$ is odd,
\[
v'=\vt\bigl(3^{a'+2}+5\bigr)-2
=\vt\bigl(a'+2-\mathrm{Log}_3(-5)\bigr):
\]
\emph{the survival reading is the $2$-adic alignment of the exponent
to the $2$-adic logarithm of the ghost $-5$}, where
$N_0=\mathrm{Log}_3(-5)=2556611305511861515 \bmod 2^{64}$ and
$\vt(3^N+5)=2+\vt(N-N_0)$ for $N\equiv N_0\bmod2$.
\end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[The $S$-unit horizon]\label{prop:horizon}
Along any $3$-smooth-seeded chain, the round-$j$ reading is the
$2$-adic valuation of a $2$--$3$ $S$-unit form whose term count grows
with $j$; two-term forms are LTE (closed), and the collapsed cases
reduce to alignments of integer exponents to $2$-adic logarithms of
ghosts. The deterministic horizon of the cascade is exactly the
$S$-unit term count the machinery can close --- the CIC on these
families meets the banded $S$-unit frontier (Target 1) at the level of
\emph{exponents}, where effective $p$-adic Baker theory (Yu) bounds
every reading by $O(\log)$ of the exponent height.
\end{proposition}

\noindent\emph{Cascade statistics} (one-parameter family, $n\le3000$):
survival $0.16633$ after round $1$ (LTE prediction $\tfrac16$), then
conditional survivals $0.34,\ 0.31,\ 0.40,\ 0.19,\ 0.25$ --- the
re-randomized fair-thirds coin of \cref{thm:iid}. The champion is
$n=704$, surviving $6$ rounds with readings $[8,2,2,2,2,2,3]$;
\emph{every} $n\le3000$ dies by round $7$: the CIC holds, with
explicit finite certificates, for the entire $3$-smooth slice tested.
The ghost hierarchy thus lifts one level: at the base, integers align
to ghosts ($-1$, $+1$, $-5$, \dots); at the exponent level, exponents
align to $2$-adic logarithms of ghosts; survival is a parity reading
at each level, deterministic exactly where freshness fails.

\section{Where the wall now stands}

The CIC has been compressed to: \emph{a single scalar parity sequence
--- the readings $\vt(m_i-3^{-\ell_i})$ --- cannot be even forever
along any positive orbit}. The readings are provably i.i.d.\
fair-thirds coins on Haar fibers (\cref{thm:iid}); they are provably
deterministic, and lethal in explicit congruence patterns, on the
ghost-aligned sub-families (\cref{thm:mersenne}); the counting
machinery confines any violator to perpetual fast ascent with empty
phase-diagram refuge (\cref{thm:deficit}, \cref{cor:ascend}); and the
champion data suggest violators must be exponentially large in their
survival depth. What is missing is precisely the
distributional-to-pointwise step --- the same quenched calibration
wall, now localized to one coin.

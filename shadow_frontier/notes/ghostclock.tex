\begin{abstract}
We generalize the two-clock structure of the companion note to
\emph{every} periodic ghost at once: for any valuation signature
$\sigma=(k_1,\dots,k_\ell)$ of depth $K=\sum k_i$, the phantom root
$\rho_\sigma=C_\sigma/(2^K-3^\ell)$ carries an exact clock --- alignment
$a(x)=\vt(x-\rho_\sigma)$ drops by exactly $K$ per ridden block, the
ride lasts exactly $\lfloor(a-1)/K\rfloor$ blocks, and positivity caps
$a\le\log_2(|2^K-3^\ell|\,x+C_\sigma)$. The $-1$ and $+1$ clocks are
the $K=1,2$ instances; the $(1,1,2)$-channel ghost $-19/11$ of the
Target-2 analysis is the $K=4$ instance, which resolves the question
left open there: \emph{the parity channel is metered exactly like the
$-1$ ghost}. The without-replacement depth supply extends verbatim to
every phantom root. These statements were prompted by the manuscript
arXiv:2603.11066v6 (Chang), whose relevant pieces we audited: the
repulsion mechanism, the census counting formula (after restoring a
missing hypothesis), and the amortized census constant
$R\le0.0893$ all \emph{verify} (we recompute $R=0.08824$
independently); its burst--gap calculus is an exact relabeling of our
two clocks (its Persistent Exit Lemma is our parity handoff at $h=3$);
but its shadow return-time theorem is \emph{false as stated} ---
re-entry gaps of $1$ occur, and 2-adic alignment can jump $+16$ bits
in one step --- and must be replaced by the supply bound.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}

A \emph{signature} is a word $\sigma=(k_1,\dots,k_\ell)$, $k_i\ge1$,
of depth $K=\sum k_i$; write $B_j=k_1+\cdots+k_j$. The block map is
$F_\sigma(x)=(3^\ell x+C_\sigma)/2^K$ with
$C_\sigma=\sum_{j=0}^{\ell-1}3^{\ell-1-j}2^{B_j}$, and its unique fixed
point is the \emph{phantom root}
\[
\rho_\sigma=\frac{C_\sigma}{2^K-3^\ell}\in\mathbb Z_2,
\]
a $2$-adic unit ($C_\sigma$ odd, $2^K-3^\ell$ odd). Define the
\emph{alignment} $a(x)=\vt\bigl((2^K-3^\ell)x-C_\sigma\bigr)
=\vt(x-\rho_\sigma)$. Our catalogue of ghosts is exactly the family of
phantom roots:
\[
\begin{array}{lcccc}
\toprule
\sigma & \ell & K & \rho_\sigma & \text{role}\\
\midrule
(1) & 1 & 1 & -1 & \text{the $-1$ clock (ascent meter)}\\
(2) & 1 & 2 & +1 & \text{the $+1$ clock (trivial cycle)}\\
(1,2) & 2 & 3 & -5 & \text{negative cycle}\\
(1,1,2) & 3 & 4 & -19/11 & \text{the parity channel of Target 2}\\
(1,1,1,2,1,1,4) & 7 & 11 & -17 & \text{negative cycle}\\
\bottomrule
\end{array}
\]

\section{The universal clock}

All verification counts below refer to \texttt{search/ghostclock.py},
run over the five catalogued families plus $20$ random signatures.

\begin{lemma}[Cylinder threshold]\label{lem:cyl}
$a(x)\ge K+1$ if and only if the next $\ell$ letters of $x$ are
exactly $\sigma$; the modulus $2^{K+1}$ is minimal. (Verified: minimal
forcing modulus $=K+1$ for all $25$ signatures.)
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[Universal ghost clock]\label{thm:clock}
Let $a=a(x)\ge K+1$. Then:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] (\emph{Repulsion.}) After one ridden block,
$F_\sigma(x)-\rho_\sigma=\tfrac{3^\ell}{2^K}(x-\rho_\sigma)$, so the
alignment drops by exactly $K$. (Verified: $200$ block applications,
drop $=K$ exactly.)
\item[(ii)] (\emph{Ride cap.}) The orbit rides exactly
$\lfloor(a-1)/K\rfloor$ consecutive $\sigma$-blocks and then deviates.
(Verified: $625$ cases, equality in all.)
\item[(iii)] (\emph{Height cap.}) For positive integers $x$,
$2^a\le|(2^K-3^\ell)x-C_\sigma|$, so
$a\le\log_2\bigl(|2^K-3^\ell|\,x+C_\sigma\bigr)$: deep alignment costs
height, uniformly in $\sigma$.
\end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) is one line from the affine form of $F_\sigma$. For (ii): each
ridden block requires alignment $\ge K+1$ at its start
(\cref{lem:cyl}) and costs exactly $K$, so the number of ridden blocks
is $\#\{j\ge0:a-jK\ge K+1\}=\lfloor(a-1)/K\rfloor$; if the block were
followed at alignment $<K$, (i) would force negative valuation of a
nonzero integer, and at alignment exactly $K$ the deviating bit sits
precisely where the final letter's valuation is read, so the last
letter strictly exceeds $k_\ell$. (iii) is positivity of the nonzero
integer $(2^K-3^\ell)x-C_\sigma$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The parity channel is metered]\label{cor:parity}
The $(1,1,2)^\infty$ channel --- natural depth law, pure parity bias,
the configuration shown in the companion note to evade the $g$-law
alone --- is governed by the $K=4$ clock of $\rho=-19/11$: riding $j$
blocks requires alignment $\ge4j+1$, alignment is repelled at $4$ bits
per block, and alignment $a$ at height $x$ requires
$11x+19\ge2^a$. The question left open in the two-clock note is
resolved: \emph{every} ghost channel, not just $-1$, carries an exact
run-length cap.
\end{corollary}

\begin{lemma}[Universal depth supply]\label{lem:supply}
The points of $[2^k,2^{k+1})$ with alignment $\ge g$ to $\rho_\sigma$
form a single residue class modulo $2^g$, of cardinality
$2^{k-g}\pm1$; an acyclic orbit consumes them without replacement.
Every result of the companion note's supply section (lifetime budgets,
upcrossing bound) holds verbatim for every phantom root.
\end{lemma}

\section{The census budget (imported and verified)}

\begin{lemma}[Affine census; corrected hypothesis]\label{lem:census}
Let $x_u=A+Bu$, $\beta=\vt(A-\rho_\sigma)$, $\gamma=\vt(B)$, and
suppose $\gamma\le\beta$. Then for $t>\gamma$,
\[
\#\{0\le u<2^L:\vt(x_u-\rho_\sigma)\ge t\}=2^{L-t+\gamma}.
\]
(Verified: $400/400$ random cases.) The hypothesis $\gamma\le\beta$
cannot be dropped: for $\beta<\gamma$ every $x_u$ has alignment
exactly $\beta$ and the count for $t>\beta$ is $0$, not
$2^{L-t+\beta}$ (explicit counterexample: $\beta=4$, $\gamma=7$,
$t=5$, count $0$ vs.\ $512$).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proposition}[Amortized census constant; independent
recomputation]\label{prop:R}
Let $M(K,\ell)$ be the number of primitive necklaces of compositions
of $K$ into $\ell$ parts and
\[
R=\sum_{K=3}^{500}2^{-K}\!\!\sum_{\ell>K/\log_23}\!\!
M(K,\ell)\Bigl(\log_23-\frac K\ell\Bigr).
\]
Our computation gives $R=0.088236$, confirming the bound $R\le0.0893$
of arXiv:2603.11066 (the tail beyond $K=500$ is negligible by the
Chernoff envelope). Against the contraction budget
$2-\log_23=0.415$ bits per letter this is a $4.70\times$ margin.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}
In our language: the $K\le2$ families \emph{are} the two clocks ---
the renewal baseline. \cref{prop:R} says that all deeper ghost
channels combined, weighted by shadow density $2^{-K}$ and per-letter
gain, supply at most $0.088$ bits of drift advantage: an
\emph{amortized} complement to the per-family supply bounds of
\cref{lem:supply}. The orbit-level application (that a single orbit
cannot beat this amortization) is precisely the calibration wall; the
structural sum itself is unconditional arithmetic.
\end{remark}

\section{The burst--gap calculus is the two clocks}

The manuscript's coordinates translate exactly onto ours: its run
length $k=\vt(n+1)$ is our $-1$ depth $g$; its ``bursts'' are our
$1$-runs, its ``gaps'' our descent blocks; its modular laws
($k\sim2^{-(j-1)}$, mean $3$; gap $\sim$Geometric$(\tfrac12)$, mean
$2$) are our natural meter laws; its mean-gap threshold
$g_*\approx1.71$ is our crossing constant $1/\log_2(3/2)=1.7095$; and
its two open hypotheses (mean gap $\ge g_*$; mean burst $\le2$) are
literally the two channels of our joint calibration conjecture.

\begin{proposition}[Their Persistent Exit Lemma is the parity
handoff]\label{prop:pel}
For $x\equiv27\pmod{32}$ (depth-$2$ run end in the persistent class):
the run ends in one $b{=}1$ step at $y$ with $h=\vt(y-1)=3$
\emph{exactly}, whence by the $+1$ clock a single $b{=}2$ letter
followed immediately by a new $1$-run --- ``gap length exactly one.''
(Verified: $4000/4000$.) Proof: $x=32s+27$ gives
$y=48s+41$, $y-1=8(6s+5)$ with $6s+5$ odd, so $h=3$; $h$ odd is
precisely the odd-parity handoff of the companion note.
\end{proposition}

Its compatible-class census also reconciles: enumerating odd residues
modulo $2^{2D+3}$ whose first $D$ letters lie in $\{1,2\}$ gives
exactly $2^{2D+2}(3/4)^D$ classes (verified $D\le8$), i.e.\ density
$(3/4)^D$ --- their $|C_D|=2\cdot3^{D-1}$ is the same density in a
coarser modulus convention.

\section{Refutation: the shadow return-time theorem}

The manuscript asserts (its Thm.~on shadow return times) that after an
orbit exits the depth-$K$ shadow of a family of length $\ell$, it
cannot re-enter for $\ell+K-1$ steps, the proof resting on the claim
that $2$-adic alignment recovers at most one bit per step. Both fail:

\begin{itemize}
\item Measured over $400$ random orbits per family, with visits
counted ride-aware (a maximal run of consecutive ridden blocks is one
visit): minimum exit-to-re-entry gap $=1$ for all three families
tested ($\sigma=(1,2),(1,1,2),(2,1)$), with $17$--$24\%$ of gaps below
$K-1$.
\item Alignment is not $1$-Lipschitz along orbits: one-step alignment
\emph{gains} of $+13$, $+15$, $+16$ bits were observed for
$\rho=-1,-19/11,-5$ respectively. (The mechanism: a step maps
alignment-to-$\rho$ to alignment-to-the-preimage-root
$\rho^{-b}=(2^b\rho-1)/3\ne\rho$, so no per-step monotonicity
exists.)
\end{itemize}

\noindent The correct statement available is supply-side, not
speed-side: re-entries at depth $g$ consume the band pool of
\cref{lem:supply} without replacement; nothing constrains \emph{how
soon} the next pool point is hit, only \emph{how many times} it can
ever be.

\section{Position}

The manuscript's Route-B core, the Carry Independence Conjecture ---
every $n_0>1$ eventually takes a letter $b\ge3$ --- is the extreme
special case $\mathbb P[h\text{ odd}]\equiv1$ of our joint calibration
conjecture (its Cantor set $\mathcal C_\infty$ consists of the orbits
all of whose $+1$-handoffs are odd forever). The balanced
(bounded-discrepancy) core of $\mathcal C_\infty$ is already excluded
unconditionally by repetition rigidity and the narrow-band corollary
($\sup_j|S_j|<1.246$), which the manuscript leaves conditional. Its
terminal wall --- ``distributional-to-pointwise promotion'' --- is our
quenched calibration wall, reached independently. What we import,
after verification: the universal repulsion mechanism
(\cref{thm:clock}), the census lemma (\cref{lem:census}, with the
restored hypothesis), and the amortized constant
$R=0.0882$ (\cref{prop:R}); what we contribute back: the exact ride
cap, the supply bounds replacing the false return-time theorem, and
the Lean-verified rigidity underlying the narrow-band exclusion.

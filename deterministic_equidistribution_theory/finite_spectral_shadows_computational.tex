\begin{abstract}
This companion documents the C numerical suite \texttt{collatz\_tests} and the
nine experiments (A--I) supporting the main paper, \emph{Finite Spectral Shadows
for the Collatz Valuation Cocycle}~\cite{main}. It reports the suite architecture, the exact
construction of the finite residue--height operators and their Arnoldi spectral
solver, the per-experiment pre-registrations and results, and a dedicated
analysis relating the empirically detected ``shadow'' windows to the operator
gaps. All figures are reproducible from the recorded runs. Section and equation
references of the form ``main, \S X'' point to the main paper. Throughout, the
goal is diagnostic: to test whether the deterministic defect cocycle
$W_{n,\ell}(x;\chi,s)$ is the right finite detector and where it sees structure.
\end{abstract}

\section{The suite}

\texttt{collatz\_tests} is an ISO~C11 program over the accelerated odd step
$S(x)=(3x+1)/2^{b}$, $b=v_2(3x+1)$. Build profiles:
\textsf{FAST128} (\texttt{\_\_uint128\_t}, overflow-flagged),
\textsf{GMP} (\texttt{mpz\_t}~\cite{gmp}; large seeds, exact congruence checks),
\textsf{DEBUG} (invariant assertions: $3x+1=2^bx'$, odd output, $b\ge1$).
Modules: \texttt{bigval} (the two backends), \texttt{orbit\_scan} (streaming),
\texttt{modarith}, \texttt{characters} (additive $\chi_k(a)=e(ka/Q)$ and
multiplicative on $(\Z/3^r)^\times$), \texttt{defect\_sums} ($W_{n,\ell}$ via
complex prefix sums), \texttt{entropy\_stats}, \texttt{critical\_events}
(excursions, segment labels), \texttt{residue\_height\_graph} (operator builder),
\texttt{sparse\_matrix} (CSR + Arnoldi), \texttt{io\_csv}, \texttt{config}.

\paragraph{Design requirements (adopted from review).}
(1) the $\bmod 3^r$ congruence (main, Lemma~1) is a correctness \emph{gate}
(Exp.~H), verified before any spectral code relies on it; (2) annealed and exact
spectral radii are reported in \emph{separate} columns and never substituted
(Exp.~F); (3) the shadow search reports per-window magnitude \emph{and} length so
both $\limsup$ plateaus and long sparse blocks are visible (Exp.~I); (4) the
quenched pressure is computed as $\limsup_n\frac1n\sum s\Delta_{b_i}$, not an
ill-defined sum over words.

\paragraph{Provisional criticality.} The project-internal ``$C_A$-critical''
certificate is not fixed; the suite offers \texttt{--crit-mode} with
\texttt{vsq} (the literal $V^2\le2^AP$, which is degenerate) and \texttt{height}
(peak $\ge$ launch$\cdot2^A$, the default). \textbf{All conclusions tagged
\texttt{critical\_C\_A}, \texttt{high\_ascent}, \texttt{post\_exit} (Exp.\ B, D,
I) are exploratory until that certificate is fixed.}

\section{Operators and the spectral solver}

The finite operator $\Lcal_{s,Q,L,\chi}$ acts on states $(a,z)$, $a\in U_Q$,
$z\in\{0,\dots,L\}$, with the source-twist convention of the main paper. The
residue map is forward, $a'=(3a+1)2^{-b}\bmod Q$ for odd $Q$ ($2$ invertible),
and inverse-branch $a'=3^{-1}(2^ba-1)$ for $Q$ coprime to $3$; the height updates
$z'=z+\lfloor R(\log_2 3-b)\rceil$ with killing off $[0,L]$.

\paragraph{Height coupling.} \texttt{--height-mode sync} (default, faithful) uses
one valuation $b$ for both residue and height; \texttt{factored} builds
$\Lcal_{\mathrm{res}}(\chi)\otimes T_{\mathrm{height}}$ with an independent height
valuation. The main paper shows \texttt{sync} is the correct vehicle and
\texttt{factored} is a diagnostic only.

\paragraph{Arnoldi solver.} Power iteration fails on the killed-height operator
(a near-degenerate top cluster from the height-transport mode: residuals
$3$--$9\%$, and spurious gap ratios $>1$ violating Wielandt). It is replaced by a
restarted complex Arnoldi~\cite{arnoldi1951,saad2011}: complex Givens rotations, a Hessenberg single-shift QR
eigensolver (Wilkinson shift, deflation), modified Gram--Schmidt with one
reorthogonalisation, Krylov dimension grown to convergence. Validated against
known spectra (real/complex diagonal, cyclic permutation = roots of unity,
agreement with power iteration). All operator runs then converge to residual
$<8\times10^{-11}$ with no Wielandt violations.

\section{Experiments A--I}

\subsection*{H --- congruence gate}
Verifies main, Lemma~1.
\begin{findingbox}{Verified}
$23{,}905{,}968/23{,}905{,}968$ checks match (FAST128, seeds $3$--$10^6$, $r\le8$,
$0$ overflow) and $144/144$ under GMP for a $16$-digit seed at $r=12$, $n\le155$
(values $\gg2^{128}$).
\end{findingbox}

\subsection*{A --- height-tail \& critical census}
Pre-registered: $\log_2\Pr(h\ge H)\approx-H+C$. Octave $24$, $10^5$ seeds: the
per-step slope over $H=3,\dots,11$ averages $\approx-0.97$. \textbf{Confirmed.}

\subsection*{B --- empirical Cram\'er-tilt law}
Pre-registered: critical-excursion bulk $\to p^{(1)}_b=3\cdot4^{-b}$,
entropy/digit $\to H_2(3/4)=0.8113$.
\begin{findingbox}{Direction confirmed (provisional labels)}
Octave $26$, $2\times10^5$ seeds, boundary-excluded \emph{middle} segments
($1.0\times10^5$ segs): $\hat p=(0.657,0.229,0.089,0.021)$,
$\KL{\hat p}{p^{(1)}}=0.093\ll\KL{\hat p}{p}=0.191$, entropy/digit $0.870$.
\end{findingbox}

\subsection*{C --- defect windows}
$|W|$ at $s=0$ decreases with window length ($0.31$ at $L{=}16\to0.11$ at
$L{\approx}116$, $Q=27$). Single-orbit windows are short (orbits terminate), so
the asymptotic decorrelation is read from the operators (E/F), not single orbits.

\subsection*{D --- post-exit taboo (provisional)}
$6{,}496$ post-exit windows: $\KL{\hat p_{\rm post}}{p}=0.177\ll
\KL{\hat p_{\rm post}}{p^{(1)}}=0.576$, mean $M_{\rm post}(1)=0.989<1$. Post-exit
relaxes toward annealed, away from the critical tilt. (Conditional on the
working criticality definition.)

\subsection*{E/F --- finite operator gaps}
\begin{findingbox}{Gaps present; exact $\ne$ annealed; constant by conductor}
Residue-only: every nontrivial sector gapped; the $\chi_2$ ratio equals the
closed form $\tfrac{2^{1+s}-1}{2^{1+s}+1}$ (main, Prop.) to the digit. Exact vs
annealed Fourier (48 sectors): $\mathrm{corr}=0.38$, mean deviation $0.25$---the
exact gap is \emph{not} the i.i.d.\ value. On the faithful \texttt{sync} operator
after the coboundary quotient, the gap ratio is $r$-independent and organised by
conductor (main, Prop.\ on autonomy):

\toprule
conductor & $3\,(\chi_2)$ & $9$ & $27$ & $81$ & $243$\\
order & $2$ & $6$ & $9$ & $54$ & $81$\\
gap ratio ($s{=}0$) & $1.0000$ & $\mathbf{0.9069}$ & $0.8530$ & $0.8261$ & $0.7306$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
each entry equal across $Q\in\{9,27,81,243\}$; worst non-coboundary sector at the

\end{findingbox}

\subsection*{G --- trace/determinant shadows}
Small $(Q,L)$: $\tr(\Lcal^1)=0$, $\tr(\Lcal^2)=1.25$, $\tr(\Lcal^4)=0.594$;
$\det(1-z\Lcal)$ smooth and positive---no spurious peripheral structure.

\subsection*{I --- quenched spectral-shadow search (provisional labels)}
Shadow flag: $\max_{Q,\chi,s}|W|\ge\delta$ \emph{and} $\ge3\times$ a length-scaled
null. Over $1{,}254{,}232$ windows, $4.2\%$ flag; by label
$\texttt{critical\_C\_A }8.4\%>\texttt{high\_ascent }5.4\%>\texttt{post\_exit }3.2\%$;
shadow rate grows with length to $100\%$ at length $\ge64$ ($n=218$).

\section{Relating shadows to operator gaps}

We join the flagged shadow windows (moduli $\{9,27,81,243\}$, all powers of $3$,
multiplicative characters) to the residue-only operator gaps (Exp.~F) on
$(Q,\chi\text{-index},s)$.

\begin{findingbox}{Tilt-level correspondence (clean); sector-level confounded}
Of $53{,}158$ shadow windows, $82\%$ (and $100\%$ of the $218$ long ones) sit at
the critical tilt $s=1$, where F's gaps are weakest; long shadows sit in
weaker-gap sectors (mean gap ratio $0.73$ vs.\ $0.57$). But
$\mathrm{corr}(|W|,\text{gap})=+0.25$ only: $94\%$ of shadows pick the smallest
modulus $Q=9$ (six unit residues), so the per-sector signal is dominated by
finite residue-class inflation, not the asymptotic gap.
\end{findingbox}

\noindent\textbf{Interpretation.} The finite operator decorrelates every
non-coboundary sector; the long shadows track it at the tilt level and in gap
magnitude, but the per-orbit signal is confounded by small-$Q$ finite size---the
circularity leak made explicit. This reinforces that the operator gap (not the
single-orbit Weyl sum) is the reliable object, and that the faithful \texttt{sync}
operator on the coboundary quotient is the right vehicle (main, \S Refined target).

\section{Reproducibility}

Every output is append-only CSV with a provenance header (git commit, compiler,
build profile, command line, UTC timestamp). The large-scale sweep is regenerated
by a single driver script; the operator runs use the Arnoldi solver above. AI
provenance for the development is tracked with \texttt{ailog}. Full quantitative
results are tabulated in \cref{app:data}.

\section{Addendum: the threshold-Schur and root-ball campaign}

The analytic endgame of the main paper (threshold Schur at $m=c$; bounded
shells; cascade contraction; class-IV root-ball analysis) was driven by a
second suite of audits, archived under \texttt{c\_suite/analysis/}. For
each script we list the headline finding; full provenance is in the
\texttt{ailog} record (50 captures, validation passing).

\begin{itemize}
\item\texttt{threshold\_class\_audit.py} --- exact four-class
decomposition of the $G_c$ Schur rows at $m=c$ ($c=4,5$, full word
enumeration): class I machine-zero; twisted near-locked class controls
($58\%/48\%$).
\item\texttt{cascade\_row\_audit.py} --- heavy-row ($x=-1$) cascade audit
with the two-point closed form; $\Lambda_c=1.0392,1.0051,0.9827$
($c=5,6,7$): crossing below $1$ at $c=7$; signed/abs $\to0.96$;
off-representative mass collapsing.
\item\texttt{cascade\_operator.py} --- scaled two-channel cascade chain to
$c=30$: bulk per-level Perron radius exactly $3/4$; Perron vector a point
mass at the locked state; model/measured agreement $0.5\%$.
\item\texttt{opflat\_levelwise.py} --- exact Plancherel shells of the
renewal constants ($L\le7$): primitive shells constant $\approx1.4$;
levelwise maximum grows along the dyadic-resonant direction
$u/3^L\to3/4$ ($L^\infty$ false, $L^2$ true).
\item\texttt{degmin\_mass\_audit.py}, \texttt{degmin\_validate.py} ---
degenerate-minimum mass law; classifier validated against exact sums
(machine zeros vs $|S|/\varphi\in\{1/2,1\}$ exactly).
\item\texttt{classIV\_levelcost.py} --- level-cost law per nominal shell:
$\Pr[h\ge1\mid d]\approx0.13$, conditionals $0.30,0.33$; first level
$a$-independent without exception; singleton shells cannot degenerate
($8.9\times10^5$ pairs, zero events).
\item\texttt{cellsum\_gauss\_audit.py} --- quadratic valuation
$v_3(\mathcal L')=d+i_{\min}$ on $300/300$ class-IV points.
\item\texttt{descent\_cancellation.py} --- no phase cancellation on
degenerate pairs ($|S_{\rm deep}|/m_{\rm deep}\approx0.9$--$1.0$): their
control is purely probabilistic.
\item\texttt{midstrata\_c9.py} --- the decisive $c=9$ spectrum: Fubini
density $0.3\cdot3^{-h}$ with successive ratios exactly $\tfrac13$; the
root-ball law.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Headline analytic constants established in the main paper:
pinning $C_0=\prod_j(1-2^{-2\cdot3^j})^{-1}<1.355$; shell bound
$\tilde Q_L\le3C_1<5.16$, $C_1=\prod_j\frac{1+2^{-2\cdot3^j}}{1-2^{-2\cdot3^j}}$;
level-cost $\lambda=\frac47<3^{-1/2}$; cascade and class-IV row rates
$(\sqrt3/2)^c$; asymptotic gap $1-(\sqrt3/2)^{1/2}\approx0.069$ at $s=0$.
